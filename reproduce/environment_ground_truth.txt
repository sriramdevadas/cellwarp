## FreeType (figure rasterisation only)
matplotlib links its vendored FreeType 2.6.1 in this .venv, which is what makes the
deposited figure files reproducible. A conda environment satisfying the pins above may
link a system FreeType instead; text extents then measure differently and the figure
bytes move, while every gated value stays put. See "Figure rasterisation and
reproducibility" in reproduce/figure_script_map.md.
